# Review evidence: selected traces

This package publishes redacted projections of selected **work-task** events for the paper *Review Creation, Receipt, and Adoption in Yohaku Agent Workflows*. It contains 43 indexed trace rows (T1-A, T2-A, T2-B, T2-C), the case audit and the 73 projections referenced by that audit. Round 2/3 are archive labels; earlier rounds are outside the paper. Other trial details and the complete original archive are not included.

The host preset one lead per stage, permissions, a shared group budget, inputs and control expectations. Creating or contacting a peer, completing a check, reading or adopting feedback, and re-reading after a modification are separate observations. An assigned relationship, an invitation, self-test or final product success does not establish peer review. T2-B retains the after_seq cursor and version distinction; T2-C records a common-input defect as a possible contributing factor, not an established cause. Engineering stop testing is not observed autonomous stopping in the formal trials. The audit also preserves failure, unknown and missing observations.

The included files are redacted projections, not original complete chats or a claim of a replayable private world. Source IDs are pseudonyms; original raw-file and line SHA-256 values are anchors to the retained private archive. Those hashes alone do not enable a public reader to reconstruct or independently authenticate unavailable originals. Times are recorded host observations, not independent timestamp attestations. For the exact quotations in context see the paper's Appendix C.

Run `python3 research/review-evidence/verify.py` from the repository root. The verifier checks checksums, 43 trace-to-projection source/line links, audit references, schema and a limited local privacy-pattern scan. It cannot prove every interpretation or guarantee complete anonymization. No model, internet access or original private data is required.
