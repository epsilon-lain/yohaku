# No-stabilization Stable-AC reduces to two ordinary terminal targets
Native umbrella elementary method, not a literature-priority claim. This arose from actually reading the frozen official stable_core.py in the application inspection artifact, not from a new search result.

## Exact scope
Let s be a balanced rank2 presentation of the trivial group. Let d14(s,t) denote unconstrained primitive distance in the frozen ordinary fourteen-move graph, using exact ordered freely reduced relators. Put T=((1,),(2,)) and U=((2,),(1,)). In frozen sac-r8-v1, consider only paths to the EMPTY target that never execute move14 (stabilization). All declared moves remain atomic; relator/generator permutations are not free. This proposition concerns move applicability and primitive step count, not arbitrary cumulative resource caps.

Then the minimum length of a no-stabilization Stable path is exactly
  2 + min(d14(s,T), d14(s,U)).
Both terms may be infinite.

## Proof
Before the first destabilization the rank stays2. The only applicable non-rank-changing frozen moves at rank2 are precisely IDs0..13. The official destabilization guard requires some relator to equal the positive singleton (g,), and the other relator to contain neither g nor -g. Thus the other freely reduced relator uses only the remaining generator h and equals h^p. The group's triviality is invariant under ordinary AC moves. After eliminating g, the group is <h | h^p>, which is trivial exactly when p=+1 or -1. Hence the pre-destabilization state is a signed, ordered basis; at least one sign is positive.
At rank1, with stabilization excluded, multiplication has no distinct other relator, conjugation is a self-loop on the singleton, inversion only changes its sign, and emptying the presentation requires one more destabilization. A negative remaining singleton requires at least one inversion. Move that inversion before the first destabilization instead: it is a legal ordinary inversion on the other rank2 relator. This yields T or U after at most L-2 ordinary moves from any length-L no-stabilization solution. Thus L>=2+min(d14(s,T),d14(s,U)).
Conversely, reach T or U by an ordinary path and append two legal destabilizations: T+[16,15] and U+[15,15] both reach EMPTY. This proves the reverse bound. No generator swap was inserted as a free move: U is simply a different legal terminal state before paid destabilizations.

Equivalently, assigning each signed basis a terminal cost2 plus its number of negative signs gives the same minimum; ordinary inversions turn it into the positive basis in exactly that many available steps.

## Why it can help this round
The external certified ordinary distances to T are8 for ac-08491 and13 for ac-07445. Their frozen Stable witnesses have lengths10 and15. A strictly shorter Stable route therefore must EITHER use at least one stabilization OR, if using none, reach U in fewer than8/13 ordinary steps. This is a search-target reduction/dichotomy, not a lower bound on all Stable paths and not a claim that U is close. It suggests searching the swapped ordered terminal explicitly rather than repeatedly proving the already solved distance to T. It does not require the theory direction to abandon its independent concept-cost topic.

Every actually proposed improved path must pass the unchanged official checker including all intermediate states. No shorter witness or global Stable optimality is asserted here.
